Pocket Decks is played in the Android app.

Make your own beats, mix them live. A beat lab for building grooves, two decks for mixing them, and a road of gigs from a bedroom stream to the Main Stage.

The Beat Lab. A 16-step sequencer with eight lanes, four synthesized kits, tempo and swing, eight save slots, and chaining to build beats into a full track. Every sound is synthesized on the phone: no samples. Share a beat as a code or file and import it on another phone.

The decks and the gigs. Two decks with sync, loops, stutter, cue, a filter, a crossfader, echo and a riser. Six venues with a crowd to win; record any set and keep it in Your sets on the phone.

  • Free to play through the first two gigs, with the Beat Lab fully open. One purchase unlocks the other four venues, three more kits, the Lead lane and the extra effects; nothing else to buy, no ads, no accounts.
  • Fully offline. Portrait, touch-first, with sound, reduce-motion and larger-text settings.
  • Your beats, sets and progress save on the device; beats and sets leave the phone only when you export them yourself.
