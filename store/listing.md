## Full description
**Make your own beats, mix them live.** Pocket Decks is a DJ game in your pocket: a beat lab for building grooves, two decks for mixing them, and a road of gigs from a bedroom stream to the Main Stage.

**The Beat Lab.** A 16-step sequencer with eight lanes (kick, snare, clap, hi-hat, open hat, perc, bass, lead), four synthesized kits, tempo and swing, eight save slots, and chaining to build beats into a full track. Every sound is synthesized on the phone with Web Audio: no samples. Share beat turns any beat — or the whole Lab — into a code or file you can send to another phone; Import beat brings it back.

**The decks and the gigs.** Two decks with play, sync, loop, stutter, cue, a spring-loaded filter, a crossfader, echo and a riser. Six venues, each with a crowd to win and three stars: clean blends and drops landed on the 1 lift the crowd; a tired track or two clashing beats lose it. Record any set — every recording is kept in Your sets on the phone, to play, rename, delete, save or share. Clearing venues opens kits, lanes and effects and climbs the ranks from Bedroom DJ to Headliner.

- Free to play through the first two gigs, with the Beat Lab fully open. One purchase unlocks the other four venues, three more kits, the Lead lane and the extra effects; nothing else to buy, no ads, no accounts.
- Fully offline. Portrait, touch-first, with sound, reduce-motion and larger-text settings.
- Your beats, sets and progress save on the device; beats and sets leave the phone only when you export them yourself.
