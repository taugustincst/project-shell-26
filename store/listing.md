## Full description
**A DJ game and beat maker in your pocket: build your own beats, mix them live on two decks, and play your way from a bedroom stream to the Main Stage.**

**Make beats in the Beat Lab.** A 16-step drum machine and sequencer with eight lanes (kick, snare, clap, hi-hat, open hat, perc, bass and lead), four kits, tempo and swing, eight save slots, and chaining to build beats into a full track. Every sound is synthesized on the phone as it plays; there are no samples. Share beat turns a beat, or your whole Lab, into a code or a file for another phone, and Import beat brings it back.

**Mix them live.** Two decks with play, sync, loop, stutter and cue, a spring-loaded filter, a crossfader, echo and a riser. Hold the filter to build tension and let go on the 1 for a drop; slide the crossfader for a clean blend.

**Win the crowd.** Six venues, from a bedroom stream to a rooftop party, a warehouse, a pier festival, a midnight club and the Main Stage, each with a crowd to win and three stars to earn. Clean blends and drops landed on the 1 lift the room; a tired track or two clashing beats lose it. Clearing venues opens kits, lanes and effects and climbs the ranks from Bedroom DJ to Headliner.

**Keep your sets.** Record any set. Every recording is kept in Your sets on the phone, to play, rename, delete, save as a file or share.

- Free to play through the first two gigs, with the Beat Lab fully open. One purchase unlocks the other four venues, three more kits, the Lead lane and the Stutter and Riser effects; nothing else to buy.
- No ads, no accounts.
- An offline music game: no connection needed, ever.
- Portrait and touch-first, with sound, reduce-motion and larger-text settings.
- Your beats, sets and progress save on the device; beats and sets leave the phone only when you export them yourself.
